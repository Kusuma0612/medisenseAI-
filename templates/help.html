How to use the workspace

1. Enter text

Use a short, clear, de-identified sample. Do not enter personal identifiers.

2. Save a session

Duration and severity are optional context fields, not model inputs in this version.

3. Review and export

Use History to review or delete sessions and Reports to export a PDF copy.

4. Model outputs

Predictions will become available only after the three trained models are integrated and tested.
